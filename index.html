StressFree Student

Our Mission:

Welcome to StressFree Student! Our mission is to help students struggling with study-induced stress.Right now, up to 27% of teens suffer from school-related burnout. During exam season, it's easy to fall victim to overwhelming stress that actually inhibits your ability to study. Neglecting your mental health during this time only exacerbates that stress. Our website is designed to bridge the gap between academics and self-care, helping you study effectively for exams while prioritizing your wellbeing. Burnout can actually undermine cognitive development and performance over the long term. We want students to use our product as a substitute for turning to social media as a stress reliever. Contrary to what many believe social media can actually exacerbate stress felt during exams. We hope our product will aid stressed students who want to study well and care for their mental health.

What is burnout?

Burnout is a state of extreme physical and mental exhaustion caused by overworking yourself or neglecting mental health during a period of ongoing stress.

5 symptoms of burnout:

  • Feeling tired despite having slept enough
  • Brain fog
  • Physical pain (like headaches)
  • Feeling withdrawn
  • Reduced performance

How to Prevent Burnout?

Preventing burnout is a lot easier said than done! By giving yourself breaks and making time for rest you can mitigate the risks of burnout; however, these habits are not guaranteed protection against burnout. StressFree Student is designed to help you get through the tasks you need to get done while prioritizing your mental and physical wellbeing.

How to heal from Burnout?

Healing from Burnout looks different from everyone but some strategies many have cited helped them include:

  • Prioritizing physical rest
  • Setting boundaries that protect your mental health
  • Talking with a trusted person (this could be a friend or a licensed therapist)

Reminder for Everyone:

We created this project to help anyone and everyone with mental health issues with our ‘brain breaks’ which appeal to all ages. This is just a reminder to be kind to others because you really never know what they’re going through!
